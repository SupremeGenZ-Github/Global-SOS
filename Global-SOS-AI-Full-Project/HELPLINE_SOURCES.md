## Verification process

Government pages were opened during research. Own-country sources corrected conflicting foreign guidance for Malaysia, Sri Lanka, Saudi Arabia and Indonesia. Government domain affiliations are retained; official appearance alone is insufficient. The National Portal of India supplied additional specialist categories.

Administrative review retains an exact approved source identity, evidence notes, previous revisions and a directory version. Monitors queue page changes and do not infer or publish replacement contacts. Independent authority verification, exhaustive regional coverage and operational certification remain outstanding.
