# Use and service limits

This development software is supplied under the MIT license. No emergency response, dispatch, communication delivery, country coverage or uptime is guaranteed. Contact details are government-page sourced but have not been live-tested or independently certified. Confirm the applicable country, jurisdiction, communication channel and service notes.

The assistant classifies requests and looks up contacts. It does not diagnose, provide professional medical/legal advice, or replace an emergency operator. Telephone and messaging actions are completed in external apps under the user's control. The app does not automatically dispatch public emergency services.

Deploying operators must supply their own terms, privacy disclosures, support contacts and distribution-policy review, keep source records current, and commission appropriate independent validation before production use.
